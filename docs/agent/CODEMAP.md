## Three ways in

**Record a payment.** `mobile/src/screens/invoices/InvoiceDetailScreen.tsx` calls `mobile/src/services/paymentService.ts`. That posts `/api/payments` through `api/src/modules/payments/payments.routes.ts` and `api/src/modules/payments/payments.service.ts`. The body is `packages/contracts/src/payment.ts`. Idempotency and the 2-decimal amount rule are in `ledgercraft-invariants`. Focused checks: `pnpm test:api -- src/modules/payments/payments.test.ts` and `pnpm test:contracts -- src/payment.test.ts`.

**Create a quick sale.** `POST /api/sales` is `api/src/modules/sales/sales.routes.ts`. `api/src/modules/sales/sales.service.ts` uses `businessDate` and `createInvoiceInTx`, and it requires `Idempotency-Key`. Contract: `packages/contracts/src/sale.ts`. Mobile screen: `mobile/src/screens/sales/SellScreen.tsx`. Focused checks: `pnpm test:api -- src/modules/sales/sales.test.ts` and `pnpm test:contracts -- src/sale.test.ts`.

**Keep a selected calendar day.** Chart anchors use local noon in `src/pages/AccountingDashboardPage.tsx`. Posted figures come from `api/src/modules/reports/reports.service.ts` and `packages/contracts/src/report.ts`. A receipt date regression is `src/components/payment/PaymentForm.date.test.tsx`. Auckland quote dates are `api/src/modules/quotes/quotes.transitions.test.ts`. The October chart evidence is `docs/evidence/feature-verification/2026-11-08/README.md`. Do not change those helpers as part of a docs edit.
